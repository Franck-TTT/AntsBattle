# Loading screens: warm-up limits and failures

- Warm-up limits are real seconds, the same at 30 and 60 fps. Pipelines (a first launch compiles every one cold: 20 to 50 s on a low-end Android phone), assets in flight and sounds hold the cover until nothing is pending, until nothing finished for `warmStallSeconds` (10), or for `maxWarmSeconds` at most (120 for `loading.boot`, 30 for other covers); a wait that ends early logs a warning. The loaded groups' textures and meshes are waited for in full, so nothing pops in after the cover: past `maxWarmSeconds` that logs a warning naming what is still uploading, and `stallSeconds` without progress fails the run. `minSeconds` keeps a logo up for real time (`minFrames` counts frames). `maxWarmFrames`/`stallFrames` add frame limits on top; prefer seconds.
- **Opting out of the shader wait:** `"loading": { "waitForShaders": false }` in `project.gameengine.json` lifts every cover once its groups are loaded and the world has drawn, without waiting for pipelines (objects may pop in as their shaders finish; the run resolves with `warm: 'skipped'`). The same block tunes the limits for every cover: `"maxWarmSeconds": 20` (both) or `{ "boot": 60, "other": 15 }`, `"warmStallSeconds"`, `"stallSeconds"`. A call's option wins over the project's: `loading.run({ swap, waitForShaders: false })` skips the wait for one cover; `loading.boot({ waitForShaders: true })` keeps it for the boot while other covers skip. `gameengine validate` checks the block.
- A 3D pipeline that fails to build (a broken shader) never holds the cover: it is logged (category `render`, then `loading`), `RenderStatus.failedPipelines` counts it, and the run resolves with `{ warm: 'done' | 'stalled' | 'capped' | 'skipped', failedPipelines }`. Check `failedPipelines` in a test or a dev build; a nonzero count is a bug to fix, not a slow device.
- A step that throws under the cover (e.g. the swap step's `pool.warm` on a missing prefab), or a run with no progress for `stallSeconds` (30), fails the run: the cover lifts, the error is logged (category `loading`; dev builds also show it as a red banner) and the promise rejects. Handle the rejection if the game can recover; never swallow it silently.
